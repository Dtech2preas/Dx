How to Earn — DTech Rewards (Clear & Simple)

Quick summary (read this first)

  • You earn by completing ad tasks inside Berserker rounds. Each ad view increases your pending balance.
  • Pending money is **not withdrawable** until it is approved by the admin after we reconcile real ad revenue from Monetag.
  • Enabling push notifications gives a bonus — but we never pay per push click. Instead we reward the user state + behavior (explained below).
  • We use small processing fees for withdrawals (airtime 25%, voucher 30%, cash send 40%) — see Withdrawals section.

The Rounds System (how sessions work)

There are three earning rounds. Each round has its own risk & reward profile. Start with Round 1 (highest yield) and complete the required tasks to finish the round.

Round 1 — High Yield

  • Earnings: R0.10 — R0.30 per valid ad view
  • Missions: Complete the assigned ad clicks (example: Popunder 4, In-Page 6, Direct 8 — numbers show on the Berserker screen)
  • Validation: You must spend at least 10 seconds after clicking an ad (the system checks this).
  • Cooldown: When you finish Round 1 it locks for 60 minutes.

Round 2 — Medium Yield

  • When: Opens while Round 1 is cooling down.
  • Earnings: R0.10 — R0.30 (more likely on the lower end).
  • Cooldown: Locks for 20 minutes after completion.

Round 3 — Grind Mode

  • When: Available only when both Round 1 & 2 are cooling down.
  • Earnings: R0.001 — R0.10 (low reward but unlimited clicks).
  • Limits: Unlimited clicks — use for steady earning.

Ad Sets & How Ads are assigned

Each user gets a unique Ad Set when approved. That set contains the exact ad code (popunder, in-page push, direct link, push) that will be shown to that user only. This makes reconciliation accurate and fair.

What this means for you

  • When your account is approved we assign a unique ad set to you — no other user uses the same set.
  • We store the ad set on the server and the page dynamically injects only your set. You do not get a separate HTML file — we keep everything fast and tidy.
  • This allows admin to match Monetag stats to your exact traffic when reconciling payouts.

Pending vs Approved balance — the most important bit

We use two balances so the system stays sustainable and fair:

Pending balance

Every time you complete a valid ad task the estimated reward is added to your Pending balance. This is an estimate only and cannot be withdrawn yet.

Approved balance

After admin reconciles Monetag stats (we match actual revenue), an approval process moves money from Pending → Approved. Approved balance is withdrawable.

Push notifications — FAQ & rules

Why we ask for permission

Push permission lets us send a few timely notifications to bring you back when high-yield rounds are available. Users with push enabled earn a small bonus because push traffic typically earns more.

Important technical facts

  • You cannot be paid per push click — browser push clicks open external pages and we cannot track the click directly.
  • Instead we give a Push Bonus to users who have notifications enabled and who return and engage after a push. This is safe and compliant.

Typical push bonus

When notifications are enabled and verified, you get a bonus multiplier (example: +10% — +20%) on your eligible rewards. The exact percentage is shown in the app when you enable it.

How to enable push (quick)

  1. When prompted, choose Allow in the browser notification popup.
  2. If you accidentally block, open browser settings → site settings → notifications → allow for student.dtech-services.co.za.

Health bar — why it matters

We track how often you click ads and show a health bar so you don’t over-click and reduce your CPM. The colors tell you:

  • Green — Safe. You may continue clicking normally.
  • Yellow — Slow down. You are clicking frequently; take short breaks to keep CPM healthy.
  • Red — Pause. If you keep clicking you risk reduced approvals or temporary claim locks.

We recommend staying in green or yellow. If you hit red the claim button may be temporarily disabled for the safety of the system.

Registration & verification — what we need

  • Sign up as usual (username, email, password).
  • Provide WhatsApp number (recommended) — admins use it to contact you for fast approval.
  • New accounts are placed in a review queue. Admin assigns a unique ad set before the account can earn.
  • If you register but aren’t assigned an ad set yet, you’ll be shown in the admin “approval” panel and contacted via WhatsApp or email.

This manual step helps keep bad actors out and ensure each user gets a unique ad set for accurate payout reconciliation.

Withdrawals & Certificates

How withdrawals work

  • Withdrawals are paid from your Approved balance only.
  • Before payout we generate a Certificate (a verification document) that shows gross amount, processing fee, and net payout.

Conversion / processing fees

  • Airtime — 25% fee
  • Voucher — 30% fee
  • Cash Send — 40% fee

The fee is deducted at the moment you create the certificate. The certificate shows both gross and net amounts. Admin must authorize the certificate for payout.

What we check for (don’t do these)

  • Do not use ad-blockers or automation tools.
  • Do not create multiple accounts to cheat the system.
  • Do not rapidly click without reading — the system checks for realistic time-on-page.
  • Do not mask yourself with unusual VPNs/proxies (we may flag high-risk traffic).

We prefer to resolve issues gently: accounts may be paused, or pending amounts reduced if activity looks fraudulent. Admin will contact you before harsh actions whenever possible.

Admin verification & reconciliation (how you get paid)

Daily or periodically, admin uploads Monetag stats for the exact zone IDs. The admin panel then:

  1. Matches each zone's actual Monetag profit with the users assigned to that zone.
  2. Calculates system target (actual − 20%–30%).
  3. If system target ≥ estimated totals → approve system target share.
  4. If system target < estimated totals → approve 90% of each user's estimate.
  5. Admin reviews the pre-calculated approvals and clicks Approve to move pending → approved.

You will see the pre-calculated approval amounts in your admin panel so the admin can confirm before finalizing.
