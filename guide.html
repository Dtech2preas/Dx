How to Earn

The Rounds System

Our earning system is divided into 3 rounds. To maximize your revenue, you should aim to complete the higher rounds first.

1. Round 1 (High Revenue)

  • Earnings: R0.10 - R0.30 per click.
  • Mission: You must complete a specific number of ads (4 Popunders, 6 In-Page, 8 Direct Links).
  • Cooldown: Once finished, this round locks for 60 minutes.

2. Round 2 (Medium Revenue)

  • Availability: Opens only when Round 1 is on cooldown.
  • Earnings: R0.10 - R0.30 (Lower chance of high rewards).
  • Cooldown: Locks for 20 minutes after completion.

3. Round 3 (Grind Mode)

  • Availability: Opens when both Round 1 & 2 are cooling down.
  • Earnings: R0.001 - R0.10.
  • Limits: Unlimited Clicks. No cooldowns. Grind as much as you want!

Bonuses

Referral Program

Invite friends using your unique link found on your Profile. You earn 5% of everything they earn, forever. This is paid from our system budget, so it doesn't affect their earnings.

Notification Streak

Keep push notifications enabled and check in daily. Maintaining a 7-Day Streak verifies you as an active user and unlocks special rewards.

Rules
