# Onboarding Message Drafts

This file contains the drafted messages to send to new users (e.g., via WhatsApp) after they register.

## Approved Message (Standard)

**Subject:** Welcome to D-Tech! 🚀

**Message:**
"Hello! Thank you for requesting to join D-Tech.

We are a new project and are constantly improving, so we can currently only accommodate a limited number of users. To ensure the ecosystem works for everyone, we are prioritizing members who can use the service almost every day. 📅

Please note that payouts are processed on a **weekly or monthly basis**.

If you are happy with these terms and ready to be active, please reply with a 'Yes', and I will approve your account immediately! 🤝"

---

## Variation 1: Short & Direct (For quick replies)

"Hi! Thanks for joining D-Tech. 🚀

We're currently prioritizing users who can be active almost every day due to limited spots. Payouts are weekly or monthly.

If you're okay with this and ready to start earning, reply 'Yes' and I'll approve you now! 🤝"

---

## Variation 2: More Formal (If users ask questions first)

"Hello, thank you for your interest in D-Tech.

Please be advised that as a new and growing platform, we are currently limiting access to ensure quality service. We require members to be active daily to maintain their account status.

Withdrawals are processed on a weekly or monthly schedule.

Please confirm if you accept these terms by replying 'Yes', and we will proceed with your account approval."
